## Persona 1 — Freelancer (designer, translator, developer)
- **Goal:** create invoices, send them to clients and follow up until paid, on a computer, often while working on their own projects (brief §1, K1, K2). More specific goals: I don't know.
- **What is hard (company's words):** creating an invoice takes long; some find a mistake only after sending; following up on payment is hard (brief §2, K7; problem P1, P3).
