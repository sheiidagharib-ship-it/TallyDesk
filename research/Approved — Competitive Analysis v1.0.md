## RQs this helps answer
- RQ5: Main answer — how five tools handle steps 1–6 (above).
- RQ4: Partly — tools show three ways to fix a sent invoice (edit and resend in Zoho; clone, cancel or reverse in Invoice Ninja; void in QuickBooks). It does not show how users notice mistakes.
- RQ3: Partly — shows the reminder mechanisms tools offer (count, timing, defaults). It says nothing about how much time users spend.
- RQ1, RQ2, RQ6: Not answered — tool docs show design, not where users get stuck, why invoices are paid late, or what reminders achieve.
