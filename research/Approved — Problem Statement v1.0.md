# Tally Desk — Problem Statement
Version: v1.0 · Date: 2026-11-07 · Time: 19:42 · Created by: Claude · Approved by: Nest

**Product:** Tally Desk is a desktop invoicing app that freelancers and small companies use to create invoices, send them to clients and follow up until they are paid. (brief)
**Scope:** the flow from creating and sending an invoice to getting paid. Reports, settings and the clients list are out of scope. (brief §3)
**Users:** freelancers (designers, translators, developers) and small companies of up to ten people. (brief §1, K2)

**Sources used:** brief = Approved — brief v1.0; dashboard = Approved — Dashboard v1.0.
**Evidence limit:** the dashboard says no interview analysis exists yet. Every reason below therefore comes from the company's words (brief K1–K10), the demo app (brief K11–K20) or external research (dashboard). None of it is evidence about Tally Desk users.
**Which problem matters most (K10):** I don't know. Interviews are needed to rank them.

---

## P1 — Creating an invoice is slow, and mistakes get through to the client
Reason: the support team hears that creating an invoice takes long, and some users find a mistake only after sending (brief §2, K7). In external research, common errors are a wrong amount and missing terms or contact details, and reviewers caught only 39% of planted errors (dashboard RQ1, RQ4).
Unknown: in which of steps 1–5 our users get stuck or make mistakes. I don't know (dashboard RQ1).

## P2 — Once an invoice is sent, a mistake in it is hard to fix
Reason: a sent invoice can't be edited, and the app says to void it, but the demo has no Void button (brief K16). Legally, a correction is a new linked document or a credit note, not an edit (dashboard RQ4).
Unknown: how our users notice a mistake after sending and how they fix it today. I don't know (dashboard RQ4).

## P3 — Following up on payment is manual, time-consuming and socially risky
Reason: reminders are sent by hand and written from empty fields (brief K5, K17). Elsewhere, chasing takes about 4–8 hours a month, and about a third of firms don't chase at all, mostly for fear of harming the client relationship (dashboard RQ3).
Unknown: how much time our users spend, which channels they use and how many reminders they send. I don't know (dashboard RQ3).

## P4 — Invoices are paid late, and the sender doesn't know why
Reason: some invoices are paid late, and there is no reliable data on why (brief K4, K8). Externally, payers mostly blame admin errors, disputed invoices or lost invoices, and only 1% say they pay late on purpose (dashboard RQ2).
Unknown: why our users' invoices are paid late, from either side. I don't know (dashboard RQ2).
