## Personas
Source: Approved — Persona v1.0. The personas come from the company's words (K1–K10), the demo app (K11–K20) and external research about other people. No interviews (0) were used. None of it is evidence about Tally Desk users. (Approved — Persona v1.0)

**Persona 1 — Freelancer (designer, translator, developer).** Creates invoices, sends them and follows up until paid, on a computer, often while working on their own projects. Company complaints: creating an invoice takes long, mistakes are found after sending, following up is hard. In the demo app, a sent invoice can't be edited and reminders are written by hand. (Approved — Persona v1.0)
