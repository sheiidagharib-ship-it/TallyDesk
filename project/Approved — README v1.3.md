# Tally Desk — README
Version: v1.3 · Date: 2026-11-07 · Time: 21:07 · Created by: Claude (asked by Nest)

## 1. Project
Tally Desk is a desktop invoicing app for freelancers (designers, translators, developers) and small companies of up to ten people, who use it to create invoices, send them to clients and follow up until they are paid (a made-up app for practice).
The client asked us to understand the problem in the flow from creating an invoice and sending it to getting paid, before any design.
Our current job is to find out whether the support complaints are one problem or several, and which one matters most (K10).
